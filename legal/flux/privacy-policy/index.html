Privacy Policy

App: FLUX Courier
Developer: John Vivenzio
Bundle ID: com.john.flux
Last updated: August 24, 2026

Overview

FLUX Courier is a single-player neon roguelite game for iPhone. We are committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what data is used when you play the game.

Data We Collect

FLUX Courier does not require an account and does not collect personal information such as your name, email, phone number, or location for our own servers.

  • Game progress (on device only): Coins, cosmetics, permanent upgrades, settings, and local high scores are stored on your device using Apple’s standard on-device storage (UserDefaults). We cannot access this data from our own servers because we do not operate a FLUX backend that stores player data.
  • No advertising or tracking SDKs: The current version does not include third-party ad networks or analytics SDKs. We do not track you across other apps or websites.
  • No in-app purchase processing yet: Real StoreKit purchases are not enabled in the current build. Cosmetic items use in-game coins earned by playing.

Optional Apple Services

  • Game Center (optional): If you are signed into Game Center, scores may be submitted to Apple’s Game Center leaderboards. That data is handled by Apple under Apple’s privacy policy. You can avoid this by not signing into Game Center. Local score lists still work without Game Center.

Children’s Privacy

FLUX Courier does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Progress is stored only on the device. If you believe a child has provided personal information through an Apple service linked to the app, contact us and we will help as appropriate.

Data Security

Because game progress lives on your device, it is protected by your device passcode, Face ID / Touch ID, and iOS security features. Uninstalling the app may delete local progress.

Changes

We may update this policy if the app adds features that change data practices (for example real ads, accounts, or cloud saves). The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do.
